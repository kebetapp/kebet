❓ Help & Your Rights
Your rights are protected — read them carefully
🙏 Your Rights as a House Worker — መብቶችዎ
Kebet protects every worker. These rights cannot be taken away from you.
📅
1 Day Off Every Week
You are entitled to at least one full rest day every week. No one can make you work 7 days in a row.
💰
On-Time Salary
Your salary must be paid on time every month as agreed. If it is late, contact Kebet immediately.
📱
Keep Your Phone & ID
No employer can take your phone, ID card, or any personal documents. These are yours at all times.
🛡️
Zero Tolerance for Abuse
No one is allowed to shout at you, hit you, or treat you badly. If this happens, leave and call Kebet.
🚪
Right to Leave
If you feel unsafe at any time, you have the right to leave. Kebet will help you find a safe place.
🍽️
Food & Shelter
As a live-in worker, you must be provided with proper food and a safe, clean place to sleep.
🆘
Are you in danger right now?
If you are being hurt, threatened, or held against your will — you are not alone. Call for help immediately. Kebet will support you.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What do I do if my salary is not paid on time? ▼
First, speak calmly with your employer. If they refuse to pay, contact Kebet immediately via phone or WhatsApp. We will help mediate and ensure you receive your full payment. Keep a record of all dates and amounts agreed.
Can my employer take my phone? ▼
No. Your phone and ID are yours at all times. No employer has the right to confiscate them. If this happens, contact Kebet immediately and we will intervene on your behalf.
What if I want to leave my job early? ▼
You can leave at any time if you feel unsafe. If you want to end a contract early for other reasons, try to give the owner notice. Kebet can help you communicate this and find a new position.
How do I get my salary payment confirmed? ▼
Go to My Salary in your dashboard. Your employer marks each month as paid. If a payment is marked but you didn't receive it, contact Kebet with evidence and we will resolve it.
How do I improve my profile to get more job offers? ▼
Complete your profile 100% — add your phone number, skill, and location. Workers with complete profiles get hired 3x faster. Ask previous employers for references to build trust.
What is Kebet? ▼
Kebet (ከቤት) is Ethiopia's platform connecting house owners with trusted live-in house workers in Addis Ababa. We verify workers, protect their rights, and make the hiring process safe and transparent for everyone.
