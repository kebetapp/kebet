3. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
3.1 Salary Payment: The Employer agrees to pay the agreed monthly salary of — ETB on or before the last day of each month.
3.2 Accommodation: The Employer shall provide a clean, private, and safe room for the Employee to live in at no cost.
3.3 Food & Meals: The Employer shall provide three meals per day or a meal allowance as mutually agreed.
3.4 Medical: In the event of illness or injury sustained during work, the Employer shall cover basic medical costs.
3.5 Respect: The Employer shall treat the Employee with dignity and respect at all times, consistent with Ethiopian Labour Law Proclamation No. 1156/2019.
4. EMPLOYEE OBLIGATIONS
4.1 Duties: The Employee shall perform the agreed services diligently and to the best of their ability during regular working hours.
4.2 Conduct: The Employee shall behave honestly, maintain confidentiality of the household, and respect the property and privacy of the Employer's family.
4.3 Notice: Either party wishing to terminate this contract shall provide a minimum of 14 days' written notice.
4.4 Rest: The Employee is entitled to one full rest day per week and public holidays as recognised by the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia.
5. PLATFORM & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
5.1 This contract is facilitated by Kebet (ከቤት). Either party may contact Kebet support if a dispute arises.
5.2 Disputes shall first be resolved through mutual negotiation. If unresolved, either party may escalate to the Kebet Mediation Team via the Help & Rights page.
5.3 This agreement is governed by Ethiopian Labour Law Proclamation No. 1156/2019.
